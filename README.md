OmaPress is a native publishing app for Omarchy. Write articles, add artwork and review your work in one workspace, then publish a website and feeds or prepare it for X and Substack. Your articles and images stay in a folder you own.

## From draft to publication

- **Write and review.** Work in Markdown, switch to a full-width preview and add banner artwork above your title.
- **Publish your own site.** Generate a website with RSS, Atom and JSON feeds, and publish through GitHub Pages.
- **Reach other destinations.** Prepare articles for X and Substack, with a separate review for each destination.
- **Keep writing offline.** Draft, build and preview locally without an account or internet connection.
- **Fit your desktop.** Follow your active Omarchy theme, including Familiar’s light palette.

## Try OmaPress

[**Download v0.0.2 for Linux x86_64 →**](https://github.com/tcballard/OmaPress/releases/tag/v0.0.2)

Follow the [install and first-use guide](docs/getting-started.md), then open **OmaPress** from your app launcher.

**Early testing:** v0.0.2 includes the Quattro theme and rounded-corner fixes. The app has been launched on Tom’s XPS; a fresh Familiar screenshot and confirmation of the corrected appearance are still pending.

X API publishing is experimental. The Substack browser companion prepares a draft; you review the audience and publish in Substack. An optional Gateway integration supports drafts and scheduling. See [publishing destinations](docs/distribution.md) for the current capabilities.
